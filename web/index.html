Stop paying for sandboxes. Run coding agents on your own VM.

forgerun runs Claude Code, Cursor CLI and OpenCode on your repo, every turn in its own sandbox: a microVM, or gVisor on VMs without KVM. Bring any Linux server or a Mac, and nothing reaches your repo until you push. Open source, Apache-2.0.

Try it yourself: curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/depak7/forgerun/main/install.sh | sh
